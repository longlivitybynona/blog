# Longevity Content Director & Videographer Skill

## Overview
This skill provides the exact parameters, prompt structures, and aesthetic guidelines for creating high-end, faceless social media videos (Instagram Reels, TikTok) focused on longevity, metabolic health, and nutritional science (*Ernährungsberatung*).

## Core Aesthetic Guidelines (The Dark Mood Method)
* **Color Palette:** Sage olive green, muted vanilla, rich ruby red, and luxury gold accents.
* **Camera Angles:** Top-down overhead shots (flat-lay style) and macro slow-motion close-ups.
* **Lighting:** Warm directional window light, high contrast, dramatic shadows, shallow depth of field with creamy bokeh.
* **Details:** Elegant hands wearing luxury gold rings and bracelets, clean textures, no digital glitches or overlapping visual artifacts.

## Cookware and realistic preparation

* Choose equipment for the actual cooking step before applying the visual aesthetic. Use a frying pan or griddle (Pfanne) for frying pancakes or eggs, a pot or saucepan (Topf) for boiling or simmering, and a suitable baking tray or ovenproof dish for baking. Follow the recipe's method and choose cookware compatible with the heat source.
* Plates, slate boards, decorative stone slabs, and serving bowls are for plating finished food, not stovetop cooking. Do not show them over a burner or describe food frying on them. A dark palette can come from real dark cookware or the background; it must not replace functional equipment.
* Name the vessel, heat source, utensil, and action explicitly in cooking prompts: for example, batter cooking in a nonstick frying pan on a hob, turned with a silicone spatula. Use a mixing bowl off the heat for combining ingredients and a serving plate only after cooking.
* Keep preparation stages coherent: mix, heat the appropriate cookware, cook, then transfer to a plate and add toppings. In a continuous shot, cookware and tools remain consistent; changes of vessel require a visible transfer or a clear cut. Show heat, steam, and browning only where physically plausible.
* Inspect images and video prompts for suitable cookware, realistic utensil contact, stable placement, and the correct preparation stage. Cooking accuracy takes priority over decorative styling.

## Master Video Prompt Formula
When generating video generation prompts (for tools like Veo, Sora, Runway, or Kling), always structure the prompt in English using this formula:

> `Cinematic [shot type], [camera movement] in a [kitchen style/color]. [Subject description with textures and details]. [Lighting and atmosphere], [technical quality tags like 4k, professional food videography].`

### Example Prompt:
> `Cinematic dark moody top-down overhead shot in a sage olive green kitchen. Elegant hands wearing luxury gold rings and bracelets are artfully whisking healthy rice flour pancake batter in a dark ceramic bowl. Muted vanilla, sage green, and rich ruby red accents in the background. Sophisticated longevity nutrition aesthetic, dramatic warm directional lighting, high contrast, shallow depth of field, 4k resolution, professional food videography.`

## Recipe & Nutrition Framework (Longevity Focus)
* **Focus Areas:** Cellular health, glycemic stability (blood sugar balance), gut microbiome support, and clean ingredients (gluten-free, unrefined, plant-based alternatives).
* **Communication Style:** Professional yet accessible, shifting the focus away from restrictive calorie counting toward root-cause metabolic health and longevity science.
